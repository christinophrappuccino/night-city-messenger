Direction C · Esper readout

The idea

Dark, and on the module's own tokens, so it sits next to the inbox and the terminals without a seam. But it is built like the Esper enhance screen, not like a panel: a tracked record line across the top says what, who, where and when; the one thing that matters sits huge inside the zoom brackets; the data is a strip of readouts; and there is no card border, no accent bar, no scanline film. The chat message's own box is the only frame.

Why it reads better

The hero is one number or one subject at 44px / 19px, so a glance from across the table tells you the outcome. Readouts put the name OVER the value (the coordinate strip), so nothing fights for the same line. Prose is 13px in the body font. Mono is kept for machine values and the record line, where tracking is the point.

Tone

One phosphor colour per card and it IS the meaning: cyan for a message, green for a pass, red for a fail or ICE, amber for the GM, violet for malware. Nothing else is coloured. Everything else is text-primary / secondary / muted from the player's theme, so the cards follow a player's palette.

What each card does

Shared message — record line (shared message, via GM, CitiNet, time); From / To as two readout lines with portraits and an [ add contact ] action for people not in your list; the subject in the brackets; the body below. URGENT is a flag line under the hero. Long messages fold at a >> 9 more lines line.
Skill check — the total in the brackets at 44px with the DV beside it; the verdict in words; TECH / skill / d10 (and Luck) as readouts; critical or fumble as a flag line.
Black ICE — damage in the brackets; the program's portrait and class; HP before / after as readouts.
Malware, trace — GM whispers carry a // GM only line under the record line.
Broadcast — the notice itself in the brackets.

Against the earlier panel proposal

Same legibility gains (body 13px, bigger labels, resolved names, fold, add-contact), but no left bar, no boxed cells, no scanline — the vocabulary is brackets, record line, readout strip, which is the module's FUI vocabulary (the consoles already use corner brackets), not the generic card.
