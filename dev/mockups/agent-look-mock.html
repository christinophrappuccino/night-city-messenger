The Agent look

Built around the AI, not app icons: its projection sits at the top, it says one line about what matters, content is glass slips, and you get around by asking it (the command chips). The form factor sets the layout — holo slab, clamshell, slab — and the device's theme colours it. Everyone's Agent uses this: your own (full) and someone else's (read-only, "held by"). App names come from one editable table — ZPost and BabelChat by default.

1 · Your own Agent — Rache's, a holo slab, her player theme

A player can flip their inbox between Desktop (today's window, for heavy mail work) and Agent. In Agent view everything is live: reply, call back, open a chat. Bigger jobs open the windows that already exist (the composer, the call screen, a conversation).

2 · Someone else's Agent, locked — the three forms

The form changes the whole layout. A holo slab projects its AI above the glass; a clamshell talks on its little display-only lid while you work the panel below; a cheap slab has a pixel face and no manners.

3 · Inside someone else's, and lock styles

Read-only, same screens as your own, with the "held by" strip and an AI that keeps telling you whose life you are in. The lock style rides on any form: a corp slab with its legal notice, a friend's holo slab with a photo of his wife.

4 · GM — the device's form, and the app names

Plain words here. The form and the quality are per device; the app names are one world setting every Agent reads.
