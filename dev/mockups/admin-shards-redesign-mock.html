What changed

The list comes first. Creating a shard moved into + New (blank, from a preset, convert an item, import). Player authoring is one header button that shows On/Off. Relock all, Purge destroyed and Copy player shards to Items sit in ⋯. The session-only "Live" strip is gone — the Security tab's log keeps the full history.

Built for many shards: one scrolling list, grouped by who holds it (default), who made it, preset, folder or status, under headers that fold. Rows no longer expand in place and shove the list around.

Filters with counts: locked, breached, unclaimed eddies, damaged, player-made, bricked.

The detail shows the lock as a chain (click a layer to lock or unlock it), every entry in a scrollable list with its eddies, encryption and damage at a glance, and integrity in one block.

Fixed on the way: Import JSON and the bulk "Move to…" were buttons with nothing behind them.
