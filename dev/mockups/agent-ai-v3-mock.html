The Agent's AI — v3, a real head

A realistic head model (a scan, with the 52 face-capture blend shapes phones use) given the hologram treatment. Its own mesh topology is the wireframe — like the reference. The face moves through real blend shapes: the jaw when it talks, the lids when it blinks, the brows, cheeks and mouth corners with its mood, the eyes where you are. Male only in this mock — a female (and the male we ship) need a model we're allowed to distribute; see the note.
