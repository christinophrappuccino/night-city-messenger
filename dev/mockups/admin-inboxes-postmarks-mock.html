A · THE WALL — pigeonholes that hold envelopes
Hover a mailbox: its newest three fan out (click one = that message). Click a mailbox: a drawer pulls out under the shelf — latest mail, 14-day traffic, the networks it was routed over as postmarks, who it talks to, actions. Quiet mailboxes gather dust. Press / and type, arrows + Enter. Try “Simulate delivery”.
B · THE SORTER — an intake belt, a rack of bins, a manifest
The belt carries the latest mail, newest at the right. Hover a parcel: a route line drops to its bin. Hover a bin: its parcels light up on the belt. Click a postmark in the key: only mail routed over that network, everywhere. Click a bin: the manifest on the right. “Simulate delivery” drops a parcel onto the belt.
