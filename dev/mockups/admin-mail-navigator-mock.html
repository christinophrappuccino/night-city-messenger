A · THE CALENDAR — year → month → day, each one opening into the next

Stage 1 is a year: twelve month tiles, each with how much mail (a tiny bar per week, amber where it is unread), its count and its unread. Click March and the tile grows into stage 2, the month grid: a cell per day, tinted by how much, the count in it, an amber dot for unread and a red dot for undelivered; the week numbers under it pick a whole week. Click the 11th and the grid folds up into a seven-day strip (stage 3) so you can step to a neighbouring day without going back up, and that day's mail lists under it by hour. The breadcrumb walks up; ‹ › steps sideways at any level. Search and Who sit above all of it and narrow every count on the way down.

B · THE DRAWERS — a cabinet you pull open, in place

The column is a cabinet. The top drawers are time — Today, Yesterday, This week, This month, Older — then People (one drawer per character, their mail inside), then Flags (Unread, Not delivered, Anonymous, Eddies, Junk). Every drawer front shows its count and unread. Pull one and it slides open where it is, pushing the others down; the others stay as thin fronts so you can see where you are and jump straight to another. Inside a big drawer there are smaller drawers: This month opens to its weeks, a week to its days, a day to the mail. Pull a second drawer and the first one closes, unless you pin it.
