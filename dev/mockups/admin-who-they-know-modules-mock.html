Who they know — built from modules

Admin → Contacts → Players → V. Small instruments on top, the same compact list underneath. Every module narrows the list; the chips under the modules show what's narrowing it. Try: click a ring of the orbit, a dot, a role bar, a waiting row.

  • Orbit — V in the middle; everyone they know is a dot. Inner ring = talked this week, then this month, longer ago, and the outer ring = never written. Colour = role, size = how much mail, a dashed amber ring = waiting on an answer. Click a ring to filter to it, a dot to pick that person.
  • Role equaliser — one bar per role, height = how many. The amber cap means someone in that role is waiting. Click bars to filter (several at once).
  • Waiting — who wrote and hasn't been answered, newest first, with the count.
  • Contact card — whoever is picked, as a readout: role, org, address, trust, last mail, and the buttons.
  • Each is a separate piece, so we can mix and match — e.g. Orbit + list only, or Equaliser + Waiting only — and reuse them in other tabs (an orbit for an NPC's correspondents, an equaliser for a terminal's accounts…).
