B · THE DAY RAIL — one row per message, but you can see the shape

Keeps today's row-per-message and the reading pane exactly as they are. Three things change on the left: replies nest under the message they answer (an indent and a connector, so a back-and-forth reads as one block instead of six rows scattered through the day); every row carries its sender's colour on the edge, so a glance tells you who is talking; and a MINIMAP strip on the far left shows the whole history as density — one line per message, amber for unread, red for failed — with the part you're looking at framed. Drag the frame to jump through months without paging. Day bands stay sticky.
