Who they know — 40 contacts, three ways through it

Admin → Contacts → Players → V → Who they know. Each frame is clickable; every one keeps the search box. Try searching "ar", clicking role chips, lanes, tiles and contacts.

A — Index. Pick one or more roles (chips light up), sort by Recent / Waiting / A–Z, and jump with the letter rail. The quickest way to find someone.

B — Activity lanes. Grouped by what's going on: waiting on you, talking this week, gone quiet, never written. Contacts are small chips; click one for its card. Shows 40 people in very little space.

C — Rolodex. A tile per role with faces and counts; click a tile to go into it (Back to return). Switch to Organizations. Searching skips the tiles and shows matches.
