1 · The delivery line

Admin → Messages → All mail → the open message, under From / To / When

One line that answers "did it get through?" — quiet green when fine, amber or red when not. Click it to open the why, what the player saw, and what happens next by itself, with the fixes inline.

Try: click the amber "Held" line → Deliver it now → it turns green with an undo. Same for Refused → Let players write to it. Needs: the module to start recording when a message is read.

Group mail (a contact group, several people, or sent as an alias): the line counts the party with one square per person; open it for one row each, with that person's fix. Try Deliver now on Rache or Remove from The Crew on Brick. Problem lines start closed.

2 · At the table, reworked

Admin → Overview — replaces the current At the table

Players grouped by the scene they're looking at (with that scene's networks, or that it's a dead zone). Each player: online, network and signal, and the few things that decide whether their Agent works — address, contacts, unread, mail held — with one word for where they stand.

Try: click a player to open the why and the fixes under their scene; press Set an address or Deliver it now and watch them flip.

3 · Lock odds in the account editor

Admin → Terminals → Configure → Accounts → an account → Security (700px window)

Everything above the dashed line is the editor as it is today. The new panel under it reads each character's sheet and shows their odds with their best listed skill, plus a one-line verdict. Up to 4 characters: side-by-side cards; 5 or more: a compact best-first list in its own scroll box, with the ones who can't roll folded away.

Try: change a DV, add a skill, switch ANY / ALL or the failure rule; switch the party to 7 or 12; turn Require login off and it shrinks to one line.

4 · Account status in play

Admin → Terminals → the terminal's detail → Accounts (Admin Panel width)

During the session: each character against this account — logged in (since when), locked out (live clock), tries left, or can't see it and why — each with the one action you'd want. The header shows the lock in a few words with a link to edit it.

Try: Log out, Clear lockout, Reset tries; watch Rache's clock count down. Needs: failed tries read from the Security log, and players' lockouts sent to your screen.
