NPC detail — who writes to them (the Players-tab orbit, reversed)

Admin → Contacts → Directory → Rogue Amendiares. The module sits in the NPC's detail pane (~470px). Both versions show the same data and feed the same card: who exchanges mail with Rogue, how recently, how much, who is waiting on an answer, and which of Rogue's identities they write to. Click a dot / a row to pick someone, a ring to narrow, an identity chip to see only mail that reached that address. Try the trust steps, Write as, and Share in the card.

C · Switchboard (NPC-specific)

Rogue is a switchboard, not a face: the hub is split into one wedge per identity (her own address, and Queen of Hearts in violet), sized by how much mail each one gets, and every correspondent's line plugs into the wedge they actually wrote to. Distance means how much she owes them: anyone waiting on a reply is pulled inside the amber "your turn" ring, and the longer they have waited the closer they sit; answered people drift back out (brightness = how recently). Player characters are solid dots; other NPCs are dim outlines. The small arrow on each line says who wrote last: amber pointing in = they did, it is your turn; grey pointing out = Rogue did, waiting on them. The reply queue under it lists everyone waiting, longest first, with a one-click reply as the identity they wrote to.

A · Orbit

Rogue is the disc in the middle; its outer ring shows how her mail splits between her own address (grey) and her alias Queen of Hearts (violet). Everyone who writes to her is a dot: inner ring = this week, then this month, longer ago, and the outer ring = has her as a contact but never wrote. Round dots are player characters, diamonds are NPCs; colour = role, size = how much mail, a violet arc on a dot = the share of their mail that went to the alias, and a dashed amber ring = they're waiting on a reply.

B · Exchange timeline

One lane per correspondent, the last eight weeks left to right with "now" at the right edge. A tick above the line is mail they sent Rogue, a tick below is Rogue's answer; violet ticks went through the Queen of Hearts alias. An amber box marks mail still waiting, stretching from when it arrived to now, so you can see how long they've been left hanging. Player characters first, waiting ones on top; never-written contacts get a dashed lane. Click a lane to pick someone.
