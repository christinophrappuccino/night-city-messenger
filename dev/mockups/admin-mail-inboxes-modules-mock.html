ALL MAIL · A · MAIL CALENDAR — the strip is the grouping

One cell per game day, two weeks at a time (‹ › pages further back). Brighter = more mail that day, the amber bar on top = unread, the red dot = something didn't arrive. Drag across days or click one and the list below narrows to it; the line under the strip says what you're looking at and clears with one click. It replaces Group by Day/Week/Month and the date folds — nothing else about the list or the reading pane changes. Kept deliberately simple: no month view, no heat legend, just days.

ALL MAIL · B · FLOW BOARD — who writes to whom

Players on the left, NPCs on the right, a line for every pair that has written to each other, thicker with more mail; a line turns amber while that NPC owes a reply. Click a line (or a face) and the list shows only that mail; click again to let go. The summary line names the pair. The date grouping and folds stay as they are below — this instrument sits ABOVE the list as a filter you can see, instead of the Who scope picker.

INBOXES · A · THE MAILROOM — a wall of pigeonholes

One slot per mailbox. Players along the top shelf, NPCs on shelves by role (or organisation — the Group control stays). Each slot: the face in its colour, a fill bar for how much mail is in it, an amber flag with the unread count, a red "owner gone" tag on an orphan. Click a slot to open the inbox; hover for open / export / clean up. A search dims every slot that doesn't match; the Players / NPCs / Has unread / Empty views stay as chips.
