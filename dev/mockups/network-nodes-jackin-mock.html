Getting in cracks the lock open and the network blooms out in front of you, with your moves dropping in. A miss grinds, goes red, and slams shut.

The beats (about 6 seconds, skippable)
  1. Line in. The lock fades up, three rings spinning at different speeds.
  2. Box lock, firmware. Each ring decelerates and clicks into the slot at the top (a snap, a flash along the guide, the layer turns green on the left).
  3. The roll. Tech, Interface and Deck lock in. The d10 and the last ring keep spinning; the total stalls at 12, the screen darkens at the edges and pulses like a heartbeat.
  4. IN. The ring overshoots, settles in the slot, the d10 lands: 18. Every ring flashes white, a shockwave goes out, the lock splits apart, and the network blooms outward node by node behind it. The name decodes; your moves drop in one at a time.
  5. MISS. The ring stops short of the slot and grinds. The rings go red from the inside out, a crack runs across, the lock slams shut, static, the ICE skull, the alert jumps.
  6. GM decides. The first two rings click in; the last keeps turning slowly until the GM calls it.
Sound (existing assets): a click per snap, the hack-progress hum building through the stall, hack-success on the open (shard-decrypt as the bloom), hack-fail + black-ice on a miss.
