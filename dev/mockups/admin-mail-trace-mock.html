A · THE TRACE — one histogram, state is the colour, drag to narrow

Every bar is a day (zoom to hours, weeks or months — the bars re-count, the picture never gets busier). Each bar is stacked by state: read cyan, unread amber, not delivered red, anonymous violet, junk grey. The state chips above show the counts for what's on screen; click one to drop that colour out of the bars and the list, double-click for only that state. Drag across bars to pick a range; click a bar for a day; the part outside the range dims. Who is a scope picker: pick a person and the trace redraws with only their mail. The list below is the narrowed set, grouped by day as now, paged. 1,412 messages over eight months, and the instrument is the same size it would be with forty.

B · STATE LANES — every state gets its own shape

Same counts, one thin lane per state stacked like the runway. Each lane is its own sparkline over time, so you see the states separately: the unread pile growing all week, the two days deliveries failed, the anonymous mail clustered around one night. Click a lane's name for only that state (click again to let go); click several for a mix; drag across any lane to pick a range, and it applies to every lane. Zoom and Who as in A. Six lanes stay six lanes no matter how much mail there is.
