Admin → Security → Activity — a strip of the session above the log

Two takes on the same module. Same data in both: 40 events in a six-hour session (18:00 to now, 23:55 on the game clock) from four characters, with a run of failures on the Arasaka workstation around 21:30. Your last look at this tab was at 22:40, so 12 events are new. Both versions filter the same log underneath. Click anything; drag across the strip to pick a stretch of time.

Version A — the seismograph

One line runs across the session like a seismograph needle. Each failure kicks the line up and it rings for a moment; the taller the kick, the more heat it gave. Successes are small ticks on the rail at the bottom. Above it, one row of dropdowns: Who, Target, Severity, Result and Action (the NET verb) narrow both the trace and the log, and every option shows how many events you would see if you picked it. Trace switches between one combined line, one line coloured by character, and a line per character so you can compare who caused what. Whatever is narrowing the view shows as removable pills under the toolbar. Zoom and the key sit on the trace itself; the log has its own Sort. Hover for the nearest event, click a spike for that moment, drag for a stretch of time. The amber dashed line is your last look, and the log marks new events with an amber time and a divider.

Version B — the heat strip

One lane per character, plus one for everyone at the top. The session is cut into ten-minute slices (five minutes for the last hour, one hour for the whole day). A slice turns red when someone failed in it, and the deeper the red, the more heat it gave; small green dots under a slice are successes. You can read who caused trouble and when at a glance. Click a slice to see that character's events for those ten minutes; drag along a lane to take a longer stretch; click a name to see only that character. The box under the strip says what you picked in one sentence.
