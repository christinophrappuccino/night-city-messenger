① Filter method (current)

CSS filter: contrast() on the panel — affects everything, incl. the avatar image; also drifts saturation.
Notice: the avatar photo gets crushed/blown out, and neon edges over-saturate or wash at the extremes. No per-element control.

② Palette method (proposed)

Recompute palette: push bg darker / text lighter around a perceptual pivot. Accents fixed. Image untouched.
Notice: the avatar stays clean, neon keeps its hue/saturation, and readability (bg↔text separation) scales smoothly.
How the palette method works: for each background & text color, convert hex → OKLCH, then L' = pivot + (L − pivot) × factor (pivot ≈ 0.50, factor = contrast%/100), clamp, convert back. Darker-than-pivot colors get darker, lighter get lighter — same intent as a contrast filter, but done in the palette so it never touches images/wallpaper and never shifts hue or saturation. Accents (primary/secondary/accent) are deliberately excluded so the neon identity holds at every level.
