1 — ASK ABOUT A MESSAGE

Left: the message's state as a short list — click any line to ask about it. Right: a conversation. Ask a question and the answer arrives with the next questions that make sense for this message: "Why?", "What does the player see?", "Fix it". Fix buttons really act — Deliver now moves it to delivered and the list ticks over. Switch the scenario at the top to see a different message.

2 — PREVIEW THE TABLE

Pick a scene and every player's Agent changes to what they'd see there. The little Agents are clickable (Inbox / Contacts). A fix button does the fix and the card flips to ready.

3 — LOCK BENCH

Change the lock on the left — drag the DV, tick ways in, pick what failing costs — and the sentence and every character's odds move with it. Move a character to another network to see who loses sight of the account. Try it rolls a real d10 for that character against the lock (and burns an attempt on a miss), so you can feel how hard it is before your players do.
