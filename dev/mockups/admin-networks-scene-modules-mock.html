Networks → This scene — two takes on one interactive module

Scene "Night Market". Four networks reach it. Both versions share the same data, so dragging in one moves the other. Try: drag a meter or a bubble's edge handle, click a network to pick it, flip the dead zone switch, set where players start, turn a network off.

Version A — the radio dial

Each network that reaches this scene is a signal meter in its own colour. Drag a meter up or down to set its strength (or click it and use the arrow keys). The players on that network sit on the meter as small faces, at the level they're getting. The flag at a meter's corner marks where players start on this scene; click another flag to move it. The lever on the right turns the scene into a dead zone: every meter goes dark, but the strengths you set are kept for when you turn it back.

Version B — the coverage map

A top-down view of the scene. Each network is a signal bubble, centred on its tower; the stronger the signal, the bigger the bubble. Drag the round handle on a bubble's edge to grow or shrink it. Players are dots inside the bubble of the network they're on. A dashed edge means the network is secured. Off networks become a faint outline. The list on the right gives the exact numbers and holds the switches; the dead zone hatches over the whole map.
