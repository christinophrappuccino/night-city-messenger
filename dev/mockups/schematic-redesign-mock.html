SCHEMATIC REDESIGN — interactive heist map

The floorplan IS the editor. Pick a marker type from the palette, click the plan to drop it, click a pin to edit it (label/note/type/color/scene/lock) in a popover, drag to move it (X/Y update live). A Route tool connects two pins into an infil path. Each marker can link its own scene. A locked/fog marker hides as a “?” until the crew scouts it (roll-to-unlock). The viewer renders typed icons, routes, and a legend grouped by type.
