Groups they hold — when there are a lot

Admin → Contacts → Players → V, who holds 16 groups (9 you pushed, 7 of their own) — and you have 4 more groups they don't hold. Both frames are clickable.

A — Group rack. Every group is a tile with a status light: green in sync, amber out of date, a hollow ring = their own, red = your original group is gone. Filter chips + search; your groups they don't hold sit at the end as dashed "push" tiles. Click a tile for its members (what changed, for an out-of-date one) and the actions.

B — Sync board. Built around the GM's job. A meter says how many of your groups they hold and how many are stale, with "Update all". Then each of YOUR groups as a row with a three-way state — has it / out of date / doesn't have — and the one action that state needs. Their own groups are a chip rail underneath (click to peek).
