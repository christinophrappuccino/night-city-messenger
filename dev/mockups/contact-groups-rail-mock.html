What changes. A group is a chip, not a row: colour bar · name · a stack of up to three member avatars · count (a shield when the GM shared it). The stack already tells you roughly who is in it; clicking the chip opens one tray under the rail with every member as a small card — avatar, name, role and address — and clicking a member opens that contact in the detail pane. Message all / Edit / Delete sit in the tray header, once, with Message all as the one filled button (that is the thing a group exists for). A bare address that is not in your contacts shows as a dashed envelope card.

Many groups. Chips wrap. The rail caps at three rows and scrolls past that, so the contact list under it never loses more than ~100px however many groups exist; with nothing open the panel is one line. Clicking another chip swaps the tray; clicking the open chip (or ✕) closes it.

What stays. Same data, same four actions, same editor dialog; the composer's group chip and the admin Groups card are untouched. If you would rather keep the toolbar even lighter, the fallback is a Groups ▾ button beside the tag filter that drops the same chips in a panel — but it hides the stacks until a click, which is the thing you asked to stop doing.
