The Agent's AI — a living hologram

Move the mouse anywhere on the page: the head turns to it, the eyes get there first. Leave the mouse still and it glances around on its own. It blinks, breathes, lip-syncs to what it says, and its mood changes its brows, eyelids, mouth and colour. Three builds — woman, man, gender-neutral — are one head with different bone structure (sockets, cheekbones and the hollow under them, the jaw corner, chin, brow, nose, lips, neck); hair and implant plates are options. The jaw really opens when it talks. In the build each Agent stores its AI's build, mood defaults, colour (from its theme) and name.
