Admin → Overview → Coming up — every timer in one place

Two takes on the same module. Same data in both: ten timers on the game clock (scheduled mail, trace countdowns, self-destruct mail, forged backdoor accounts, lockouts), from 3 minutes to 20 hours away. The game clock runs fast in this mock (one game minute every 3 seconds) so you can watch timers close in. Click anything.

Version A — the clock dial (half-width column, ~385px)

The game clock sits in the middle. Each kind of timer has its own ring, and each timer is an arc that starts at "now" (the top) and runs clockwise to when it goes off. The scale is stretched near the top so the next hour gets most of the room and a whole day still fits on one turn. An arc turns amber inside the hour and red inside ten minutes. Hover an arc to see its name in the middle; click it (or a row in the list) to open a small readout with what you can do about it. Click a ring's name in the key to hide that kind.

  • Fits a half column. Dial on top, the same timers as a list under it, the picked one at the bottom. Next to it the Overview's other half-width box (e.g. Recent activity) would sit.
  • Rings, inside out: lockouts, backdoors, self-destruct, traces, scheduled mail. Two of the same kind share a ring; the nearer one is drawn on top.
  • Actions are the existing ones: Send now / Change time / Cancel (mail), Call off / +15 min (trace), Erase now / Stop (self-destruct), Close now / +24h (backdoor), Lift now (lockout).

Version B — the runway (full width, ~790px)

A timeline that reads left to right: the cyan line on the left is now, and time runs off to the right. Each kind has its own lane, so you can see at a glance that two traces and a lockout are bunched up in the next hour. Zoom to the next 1 hour, 6 hours or 24 hours; anything past the edge shows as "+N later" at the end of its lane (click it to zoom out). The first ten minutes are tinted red and the first hour amber. Click a marker for the readout; the "Next up" list on the right is always the five soonest.
