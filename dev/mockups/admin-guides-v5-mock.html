1g — THE DELIVERY LINE FOR GROUP MAIL  ·  Messages → All mail → a group send

One send to several people — a contact group, or several To chips — and mail sent as a contact or an alias. The line counts the party ("Sent to 5 · 2 read · 1 waiting · 1 not delivered") with one square per person. Click it for one row each: their state and time, why when it went wrong, and that person's fix. Use a fix and that row updates.

2 — AT THE TABLE, REWORKED  ·  Admin → Overview

Players grouped by the scene they're looking at, so you see who's together and who wandered off. Each scene shows its networks (or that it's a dead zone). Each player shows online/offline, their network and signal, and the few things that decide whether their Agent works: an address, contacts, mail waiting for them, mail they wrote that's held. One word on the right says where they stand. Click a player to open the why and the fixes right there; fix something and they flip to ready.
