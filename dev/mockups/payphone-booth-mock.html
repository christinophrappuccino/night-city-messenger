Payphone booth — round 2, built on the street payphone in your screenshots (layout and feel, our own drawing). Lift the handset, slot a chip into the top slot (flavour), dial on the small touchscreen, press OK. BOOK lists your Agent's phone contacts on the screen; the four keys along the top are speed dial. SPKR puts the call on the speaker so everyone in reach can follow it. Try the two "Someone else on it" moments to see a bystander's view. Mute in the top bar.
