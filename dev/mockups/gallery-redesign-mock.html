Per-cam states: ONLINE (drift + sweep + ticking clock) · OFFLINE (static, authored dead OR cut by a player) · ENCRYPTED (contentUnlock, unchanged) · LOOPED (amber, frozen frame, stale timestamp — the heist tell).

LOOP is the marquee mechanic: a player rolls Electronics/Security Tech vs the cam's DV (SecurityConsole, the crack/siphon pattern) — success freezes the channel and persists looped:true (persistContentData, GM relay); a miss can fire the trace (traceOnFail). The GM sees the LOOPED tell instantly at the table; fiction = guards watch old footage.

CUT FEED = the harder roll (higher DV) — kills the channel to SIGNAL LOST. GM gets force-toggles per cam (the schemLockToggle precedent).

Per-cam optional sceneId → GM "go to scene" chip in the monitor view (calendar/schematic precedent) — cameras ARE locations.

⑥ PTZ CAM — steer the viewport around one huge image  ← THIS ONE IS LIVE, CLICK THE PAD

Per-cam ptz: true — the channel's image is much bigger than the frame and the viewport is a crop you drive with the D-pad / arrow keys / drag (a translate+clamp on the oversized image — display-only state, no persistence, cheap). +/− zooms between crop levels, the minimap shows where the viewport sits inside the full frame, PATROL auto-sweeps the head back and forth when nobody's driving (the idle "someone's watching" loop — any input takes over), ⌂ recenters. The GM authors ONE big establishing image (a mall floor, a dock yard, a city block render) and hides details in the corners — players find them by looking around. Optional gate: viewing is free but TAKE CONTROL needs the roll (DV) — until then you watch the patrol sweep.

Try it: arrows pan (the minimap box tracks you), +/− zooms, PATROL sweeps on its own until you touch the pad.

Real implementation: the enricher flags `ptz` when authored (or auto when the image's natural size ≫ the frame); the renderer wraps the img in a clipped viewport and a data-delegated pad drives translate/scale with clamping — same document-delegated pattern as the calendar acts, works in the grid cell AND the monitor view (bigger frame, keyboard arrows there). PATROL is a rAF oscillation, reduced-motion = static center crop. TAKE CONTROL is optional per-cam (`controlDV`) — SecurityConsole roll, and a fail can trace, like everything else.

Composer side: a PTZ toggle on the frame card + optional control DV; the authored image is just… a big image.
