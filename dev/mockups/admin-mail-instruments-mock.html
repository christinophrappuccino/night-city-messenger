A · THE RADAR — who × when, one picture

Every message is a blip. Its angle is who it belongs to — players on the left half of the dial, NPCs on the right — and its distance from the centre is how long ago it was sent: the centre is now, the rings are today, this week, this month, older. Colour is state: cyan read, amber unread, red not delivered, violet anonymous. Drag a wedge to pick a person (or two), drag between rings to pick a span, click a blip to open it in the reading pane. The sweep ticks round; a new message appears at the centre and drifts outward as it ages. Below it the list shows exactly what is inside the wedge and the band.

B · THE TUBES — a battery of mail per person

The integrity battery, turned into a sorter: one tube per person, filled with their mail as stacked segments, newest at the top, coloured by state. The tube's level says how much mail at a glance, the amber segments say what is unread, red what failed. Click a tube to narrow the list to that person; click a segment to open that message; the time brush under the tubes slides the level marker, and everything older fades in every tube at once. Players are grouped left of the divider, NPCs right; many people = the row scrolls sideways, the tubes stay the same width.

C · THE CROSSBAR — senders × recipients

A grid: senders down the side, recipients along the top, each cell counting the mail between that pair, tinted darker the more there is. Small dots say what is inside: amber top-right for unread, red top-left for not delivered, violet bottom-right for anonymous. Click a cell for that pair, a row head for everything that person sent, a column head for everything they got, the corner for everyone. The totals row and column are cells too. Hatched = a person writing to themselves (never happens). The only one of the three that stays legible with thirty people, because it is a table and it scrolls.
