Version A — the switchboard

Every NPC (and every alias the players have written to) gets one line on the board. A line lights amber and shows a count when a player is waiting on that NPC; the line with the newest unanswered message blinks. Dark lines are NPCs nobody is waiting on, so the GM can see at a glance who is quiet. The strip on top is mail per day for the last week (game days): blue is all incoming mail, amber is the part still waiting. Click a lit line to answer as that NPC, right there under the board.

Version B — the age rail

Instead of "who", this one is about "how long". Every waiting conversation is a ticket placed on a time line by how long the oldest player has been waiting — fresh on the left by NOW, overdue on the right. Three gauges count Fresh (under an hour), Today and Overdue (over a day); click one to light just those tickets. Below the rail, "Up next" is the conversation that has waited longest, opened and ready to answer; the arrows step through the queue in age order, and answering moves on to the next one.
