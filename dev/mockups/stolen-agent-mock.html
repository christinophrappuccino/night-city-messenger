Stolen Agents — every screen

Any item can be an NPC's phone. Whoever holds it gets the phone's own lock screen, then a borrowed view of the owner's life — deliberately unlike their own Agent: a portrait phone that wears its brand, an AI that knows you are not the owner, read-only apps, and a strip saying whose phone you are holding. Three brands are shown so you can see the look follow the device.

1 · Getting in

Each phone has one or more ways in, chosen by the GM: a passcode (the code can be hidden anywhere — a sticky note, a message, a shard), biometric (the owner standing next to you, alive or not, or a key item in your inventory: a cloned print film, a severed thumb, a corp ID chip), and a skill bypass against a DV. Wrong tries count down; at zero the phone locks out for a while — or, if the GM set it, wipes itself.

2 · Inside — a borrowed phone, not your Agent

One column, phone-sized, the owner's name and brand everywhere, read-only. Apps the GM did not open are greyed out. The AI keeps reminding you whose phone it is. What it can do in the first build: read mail (received and sent), open attachments and chips, see the call log and voicemail, browse contacts and save a number or address into your own contacts. Replying as the owner is a later step (shown, disabled).

3 · The GM side

Configure opens from the item's right-click (Convert to Agent / Configure), like shards and terminals. Admin → Agents lists every phone: whose it is, who holds it now, whether it is cracked, and the buttons to answer it in the fiction — lock it again, wipe it, report it stolen, give it back. Plain language here; the flavor lives on the phone.

4 · Your own Agent is an item too (a switch, off by default)

With Players need their Agent on, each character's inbox opens only while they carry their own Agent item. Its brand and model are flavor on the item; its AI name is the Agent name players already set in the Theme Customizer (one value, editable in either place, and still the window title). Lose it, and the Agent window shows the screen below; mail keeps arriving and waits. And because it is the same kind of device, another player can pick it up and try to crack it — or an NPC can take it.

5 · Every phone has its own look

A phone carries a whole NCM theme (colours, fonts, contrast — the same thing the Theme Customizer saves) and a lock-screen picture with a lock style. An Arasaka phone is a black corporate brick with a logo and a legal warning; your friend's phone is a photo of his wife and a warm everyday theme. The theme applies to that phone only, the way a terminal's theme does today. For a player's own Agent it is simply their Theme Customizer theme, saved onto the item — so whoever steals it sees their phone, wallpaper and all.
