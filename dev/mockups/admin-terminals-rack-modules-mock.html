Version A — the rack + an information-first detail (picked)

Rack: each terminal is one rack unit with its icon tile (the item's image in the module; a glyph here as the fallback), four lights — green someone is logged in, red (blinking) a character is locked out, amber a plant is armed, violet every account is locked — a small heat bar, and a red trace timer in the corner when a countdown runs.

Detail, top to bottom: the header says in one line what is going on (who is in, who is locked out, what is armed, the trace). Right now lists only live things, one aligned row each with its time and its button: sessions (Log out), lockouts (Clear), armed plants (Defuse), the trace (Call off) and heat (Clear / Escalate) — or "Nothing happening." Accounts is a plain table: the account (Admin / Hidden tags, and the person it belongs to under it), its lock in words, its status (who is in, who is locked out, tries used) and its last login with who. Click a row for the facts: who it belongs to and their address, the mailbox (messages · unread · files), the lockout rule, the last three logins on it, and Edit the lock / View as admin / Reset tries. Recent logins says who played as whom: the character, the player in brackets, the account, the result and when (the module records who and when, not how they got in). ?sel=t5 picks a machine, ?open=evidence opens an account row.

Version B — the floor map + the pick-the-lock race (not picked — kept for the record)

The list becomes a map of the city: machines sit in their district, the ones that share a network are joined by a line, a red ring pulses where someone is locked out or a trace is running, a dashed amber ring marks an armed plant, and a warm glow under a machine is its heat. A short list under the map keeps the names readable and clickable. In the detail, the lock becomes a race: each character gets a lane, the solid part is what they always bring (stat + skill), the striped part is what the d10 can add, and the violet finish line is the DV. Press Run it to roll for everyone and watch who crosses; the chance to the right is the real odds.
