1 · What players get — in the colours of the thing it opens
Three choices: Match the source, Module default, or My colours (pick them — per world as the default, or per handout when you post it). A chat card, a journal link and a hotbar icon take the source's own palette: a terminal's preset (or its custom theme), a shard's preset / overrides, an inbox the player's Agent theme. Or the module default for everything. A world setting picks the default; a single card can override it when you post it.
