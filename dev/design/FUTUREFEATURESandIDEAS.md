# NIGHT CITY MESSENGER — FUTURE FEATURES & IDEAS

**Status:** Conceptual / Future Development  
**Purpose:** Maintain a collection of potential features and systems for future development. These ideas are not currently approved for implementation.

## 1. In-World Internet & Browser

Create an in-world browser that allows players to access fictional websites, interact with digital content, discover information, and use online services.

The browser should feel like a natural extension of Night City Messenger's existing digital ecosystem.

### Website Types

Websites can use predefined types to provide specialized content and functionality.

**Social Networks**
- User profiles and identities
- Social feeds and posts
- Comments and interactions
- Followers and connections
- Integration with contacts and messaging

**Forums & Community Boards**
- Discussion threads
- Public and private communities
- Anonymous posting
- Hidden or restricted sections
- Information discovery and rumors

**Digital Marketplaces**
- Storefronts and product listings
- Vendors and services
- Purchases and transactions
- Black markets and underground listings
- Integration with the existing economy and inventory systems

**News & Media**
- Screamsheets and articles
- Corporate announcements
- News feeds
- Advertisements
- Media archives and broadcasts

**Corporate & Institutional Websites**
- Company websites
- Employee portals
- Account authentication
- Internal directories
- Restricted documents and information

**Personal & Custom Websites**
- Blogs and portfolios
- Personal pages
- Underground websites
- GM-created experiences
- Custom layouts and interactions

### Reusable Website Content

Website types should not be rigid templates. Different websites could share reusable content and functionality, including:

- Articles and text
- Images and embedded media
- Discussion threads
- Feeds
- Product listings
- Forms
- Login interfaces
- Directories
- Downloads
- Interactive elements

### Website Creation

Eventually, provide a GM-facing interface for creating and managing websites without requiring custom programming for each site.

Ideally, the system supports multiple pages, different layouts, reusable components, and persistent world state.

## 2. Application Ecosystem

Develop a system for fictional software applications that can operate within Night City Messenger.

Applications may be installed or accessed through Agents, terminals, cyberdecks, and other appropriate devices.

### Potential Applications

- Hacking and network utilities
- Security and diagnostic tools
- Communications applications
- File management
- Surveillance utilities
- Data analysis
- Specialized Cyberpunk RED tools
- Custom GM-created applications

### Design Considerations

- Applications should have clearly defined capabilities.
- Different devices may support different applications.
- Applications should integrate with existing systems.
- Rulebook-based mechanics should be verified before implementation.
- New applications should be relatively easy to introduce without modifying unrelated systems.

---

## 3. Braindance System

Explore a future interactive Braindance experience for investigation, storytelling, and information discovery.

### Potential Capabilities

- Recording playback
- Timeline navigation
- Layered information
- Different sensory perspectives
- Hidden clues and discoveries
- Investigation tools
- Saved discoveries and progress
- Integration with scenes, journals, and other Night City Messenger systems

The system should support creative GM-authored experiences while preserving player agency.

Specific mechanics and technical implementation remain undecided.

---

## 4. Surveillance & Digital Evidence

Explore ways to manage digital evidence and surveillance information.

Potential features:

- Security recordings
- Camera feeds
- Intercepted communications
- Evidence archives
- Data recovery and analysis
- Evidence modification or destruction
- Integration with security systems and Braindance

Evaluate whether this should become an independent system or part of existing security and content-management features.

---

## 5. World Events & Digital Consequences

Explore ways for events within the campaign to affect multiple digital systems.

Examples:

- A terminal intrusion creates a security event.
- A corporate contact receives a notification.
- A website changes its available content.
- A news article appears.
- A player receives an anonymous message.
- A network or facility changes its security state.

The goal is to create a more reactive digital world without requiring the GM to manually coordinate every interaction.

This should build on existing event and state-management infrastructure wherever practical.

---

## 6. Additional Ideas

Reserve space for future concepts that haven't been fully defined.

Potential areas of exploration include:

- Digital identities and reputation
- Search engines and website discovery
- Virtual advertisements and commercial services
- Digital contracts and job listings
- In-world media players
- Device-specific operating environments
- GM-authored interactive digital experiences

These are exploratory ideas, not implementation requirements.

---

## Development Principles

Future systems should:

1. Reuse existing Night City Messenger capabilities where appropriate.
2. Remain relatively independent of unrelated systems.
3. Respect Foundry VTT permissions and APIs.
4. Preserve existing world data and gameplay.
5. Support GM customization.
6. Avoid unnecessary duplication of functionality.
7. Remain compatible with the declared Foundry versions.
8. Be introduced incrementally with appropriate testing.

**Important:** This document is an idea repository, not an implementation specification. No features described here should be developed until separately reviewed and approved.
