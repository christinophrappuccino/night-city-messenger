## Architecture: chat cards — the Esper readout (2026-10-20)

Every NCM chat card is ONE family, **`ncm-cc`** in `styles/chat/chat-cards.css`, rebuilt from the dev-approved mock `dev/mockups/chat-cards-esper-mock.html` (the rejected directions stay as records: `chat-cards-redesign-mock.html` = the panel family, "too AI"; `chat-cards-receipt-mock.html` = thermal printouts, "love it, differs too much from the module"). Tester report that started it: Admin → Share to Chat posted a card headed "GM Intercept — Not visible to players" to a PUBLIC message, cut the body at 200 chars and escaped its HTML into visible tags.
- **Anatomy** (built like the enhance screen, NOT a boxed panel — no card border, no left accent bar, no scanline film; the chat message's own frame is the only frame): a tracked **record line** (`__rec`: `__kind` + `__time` on line 1, `__sub` who/where on line 2 — two deliberate lines, never a wrap), an optional `__whisper` "// GM only" strip, the **hero** inside the zoom brackets (`__hero` + `__tr`/`__bl`: a `__subject`, or a `__numrow` = 44px `__num` + `__verdict`), a **readout strip** (`__ro`: name OVER value), `__flag` lines (">> urgent", ">> critical, exploding 10"), prose in `__text` at 13px body font. **Tone = meaning**: `--ok` green, `--fail`/`--ice` red, `--gm` amber, `--mal` violet, default cyan; nothing else is coloured. Every value is `var(--ncm-*, #fallback)` — the chat log is body-mounted but the PLAYER theme writes `:root`, so a player's palette reaches their cards.
- **Five templates** (message-card / hack-result / ice-retaliation / malware-analysis / network-broadcast) + the two JS builders (`whisperCardHtml` in helpers, `postNetActionCard` in NetResolve) all emit the family; **`network-event.hbs` and `payload-executed.hbs` are DELETED** (no caller, preloaded for nothing). The old `.ncm-ice-card` chat class is gone — it collided with the Contact Manager's ICE overlay partial.
- **Shared message**: one path, `MessageService.shareToChat(message, actorId, {sharedBy})` — the player inbox AND Admin → Messages (`sharedBy:'GM'`) both use it. The WHOLE body ships as HTML (`chatBodyHtml`: HTML passes through, plain text is escaped with line breaks kept); **long bodies fold** (`splitChatBody`: >700 chars or >8 blocks → first paragraph open + a native `<details open>` for the rest, ">> N more lines" / "collapse", no JS, survives re-render). From/To are **people rows** (`__p`: portrait + name + address, directory-first via `resolveDisplayName`/`resolveDisplayPortrait`) that carry `data-ncm-email/actor/contact/name/portrait` — **never for a masked anonymous sender**. `when` = `chatCardTime(message.timestamp)` (the message's own game-time stamp in the GM's date format; every card's record line uses `chatCardTime()`).
- **Add contact is decided per CLIENT** — `scripts/integrations/ChatCards.js` (`registerChatCardHooks`, ready/114): the `renderChatMessage` hook fills each row's `__slot` with an **Add contact** button when the person is not in the viewer's (`resolveOperator()`) contacts, "in contacts" when they are, nothing for yourself / a send-only address / a group copy's joined address (`rowAffordance`, pure). A document-capture click adds through `ContactRepository.addContact` and flips the slot. Chat HTML is one document for everyone, which is why none of this can live in the template.
- Sim `dev/tools/sims/test-chat-cards.mjs` (real templates through Foundry's Handlebars + real `shareToChat`/`splitChatBody`/`rowAffordance`); probe `dev/mockups/chat-cards-probe.html` renders the REAL templates against the real cascade (needs `--allow-file-access-from-files` or the mockup server — it fetches the .hbs and loads Handlebars from the Foundry install).

## Architecture: rolls — one rule, one odds function, one panel (2.1, 2026-11-01)

Tester (Aegaeon): "12 against DV 12 showed passed by 0", "Stat+Rank+Role isn't consistent", "those rolls don't open a dialog for Luck or a modifier", "show my chances". Sim `dev/tools/sims/test-rolls.mjs`.
- **`scripts/utils/rolls.js` is the ONE rule (pure).** `beatsDV(total, dv)`: a tie FAILS (rulebook: the check has to go over the DV, the defender wins ties). `d10Odds(base, dv)` → `{pct, need}` is exact for the module's real die (2–9 stand, a 10 explodes, a 1 subtracts a d10): 100 means certain, 0 impossible, otherwise 1–99. Never write `total >= dv` or `margin >= 0` (the sim greps), and the three old `calcOdds` + `lockOdds.d10Chance` go through it. On a ladder (forge, encode) a tie is SLOPPY. Code that had no roll and must read as a pass uses `dv + 1`, not `dv`.
- **Interface = Rank + 1d10 by default** (`roleAbilityRoll` default `rank`; `stat` still adds TECH). Everything authored for Interface moved to the NET scale, about 6 lower: jack-in 9, jam 7, tap 11, backdoor 9, sweep 9, wipe 7; fallback DV 9 wherever the default skill is Interface; presets lowered; the Encoder's program abilities lowered, its alt-skill penalties raised because those alts keep their stat. Moves follow the book (1–3: 2 · 4–6: 3 · 7–9: 4 · 10: 5). The sliding DV (`dials.edge`) and the margin move are opt-in now.
- **Migration v3 asks once** (MigrationRunner, only if the world has an Interface DV and has not chosen stat): lower by N (default 6) / keep the DVs / keep the old roll. Dismissed = asked again next load. `scripts/utils/interfaceDvShift.js` (pure): a lock only Interface opens moves its DV; a lock shared with other skills gets a per-skill DV (`bypassSkillDCs`, `skillDCs`); a blank skill field means Interface on the known pairs. `ifaceShiftDone` remembers what moved so a crash can't lower twice. **Message encryption is not moved**: it is shared with other skills and has no per-skill DV.
- **The roll panel — `scripts/ui/content/RollPanel.js` + `styles/components/roll-panel.css` (`ncm-rollp-*`).** Formula parts, "beat DV n", meter + %, Luck and modifier steppers. Two forms: inline in the SecurityConsole above ATTEMPT (`rollControlsHtml` / `wireRollControls`), or `performCheck(actor, skill, { ask: { host, title, hideDV, goLabel } })`, which returns `{ cancelled: true, success: false }` when backed out. **A caller that passes `ask` must check `cancelled` before treating it as a failure.** A hidden DV writes no DV and no % into the DOM. Client setting `askBeforeRoll`. A roll with its own Luck dialog (shard breach, message decrypt, contact breach) keeps it. A new roll: `ask` + `reveal` or `deferChat`.

## Architecture: roll timing + the ROLL REVEAL (2026-10-27)

Dev: "a lot of roll checks… an animation to make it more climactic, then post to chat… make sure the other animations wait for Dice So Nice and for the animation, then post — the timing is off on some." Mock `dev/mockups/roll-reveal-mock.html` (frozen frames, `?o=&ctx=&t=&at=`), real-module probe `dev/mockups/roll-reveal-probe.html` (`?still=1` = the reduced-motion final frame), sim `dev/tools/sims/test-roll-reveal.mjs` (46). **Not live-tested; Dice So Nice untested with the real module.**
- **`SkillService.performCheck` owns the timing.** (1) **Dice So Nice** — `_showDice3d(rolls)` plays EVERY die (crit bonus / fumble penalty too; `_rollD10WithCritFumble` now returns `rolls[]`), synchronized, awaited before the method returns, capped at 12 s per die, errors swallowed; `dice3d:false` opts out. Before this the module never called DSN at all (the chat card carries no Roll). (2) **`reveal: {host, title, ctx, ok, no, verdictSound}`** plays the shared Roll Reveal in that window and posts the chat card at its verdict. (3) **`deferChat: true`** posts nothing and hands back **`result.postChat()`** (idempotent) for a caller with its own animation to fire at ITS climax. Neither → posts at once (macro/API paths).
- **`scripts/ui/content/RollReveal.js` + `styles/components/roll-reveal.css` (`ncm-rr-*`)** — framework-free, mounted into the asking window (wears its palette; body-mounted fallback is `--fixed`). One deterministic `paint(p)` fed by rAF + a wall-clock cap (the BootSplash pattern): tiles light (stat hidden for a rank-only role ability, Luck and bonus get tiles), the d10 spins and lands, a 10/1 flashes and rolls the second die, the total counts into a bar against the DV line, verdict, then `onChat`. ~2.6 s / ~3.5 s with a second die, hold 0.9 s, click skips / dismisses. Motion is VETOED by OS reduced motion or the animation pref (final frame, no spin sound). Sounds: hack-progress on the spin, hack-success/fail at the verdict unless `verdictSound:false` (callers that play their own outcome — the network dialog suppresses the adapter's via `checkSkill(…, {silent})`; terminal logins, message bypass, trace and entry decrypt keep their own).
- **Who reveals:** network/account login dialog, terminal quick logins (skill bypass, net spoof, net join), message network bypass, anonymous trace, message decrypt without the cipher grid, shard per-entry decrypt. **Who defers to its own climax:** shard breach (`attemptHack({silent})` → `deferChat`, the viewer posts after `_playHackSequence`), shard layer hacks, message decrypt grid + signal reconstruct (at the result card; attemptDecrypt with `deferChat` is also QUIET — no early sound/toast), contact ICE breach, the action console (at its verdict), the bank heist, System Sweep (its "rolled N" also waits). The Shard Encoder already reveals at the moment it posts; its lock roll has DV 0 and stays as is. **A new roll = `reveal` if it has no animation, `deferChat` + `postChat()` at the climax if it does** — never an immediate card under a running animation.
